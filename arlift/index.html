ARLIFT: Training-Free Resolution Extrapolation for Few-Step Autoregressive Video Generation

Gezhi Zhu1 · Xuemei Zhou2 · Teng Long3 · Vincent Tao Hu1

1Huazhong University of Science and Technology
2Centrum voor Wiskunde en Informatica (CWI) · 3University of Trento

Code · VibeLab

ARLIFT uses the model’s own draft of the whole video to guide generation at higher resolution. On Krea and LongLive, the same model generates frames twice as wide and twice as tall without retraining.

Method

Watch the mast first. Early in denoising, the draft guides the mast back into place. As that guidance fades, look for the boat’s reflection taking shape in the water. In the next segment, the boat follows its new position in the draft while memory helps preserve its appearance.

On bidirectional MiniMax-H3, ARLIFT uses spatial guidance without the memory step shown above.

Matched Frames

Each scene uses the same prompt and seed across methods. Read across a row to follow one method over time, or down a column to compare methods at the same frame.

Frames are numbered from zero at 16 fps. Select a frame to zoom all three images in its column by 2.5×. Select again to restore the full frames.
